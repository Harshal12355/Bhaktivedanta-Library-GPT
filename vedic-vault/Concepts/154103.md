# Perfect Knowledge According to the Vedas

> In a world governed by imperfect senses and mental speculation, humanity often struggles to ascertain the absolute truth. Śrīla Prabhupāda emphasizes that perfect knowledge cannot be manufactured by the limited human brain; rather, it must be received from a perfect source. The Vedas serve as this manual for human life, emanating directly from the Supreme Personality of Godhead and preserved through the disciplic succession. By following the instructions of the Vedas, one moves from the darkness
