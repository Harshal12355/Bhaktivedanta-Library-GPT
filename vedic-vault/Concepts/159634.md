# Bhakti Without Impediment

> Śrīla Prabhupāda repeatedly emphasizes that the material world is full of obstacles, but the spiritual energy is inherently unstoppable. When a living entity engages in unalloyed devotional service to the Supreme Lord, that service flows spontaneously, overcoming all material dualities and continuing without impediment.
