# The Master Authority of the Vedas - Śrīla Vyāsadeva

> The clarity and preservation of Vedic knowledge are due to the monumental work of Śrīla Vyāsadeva, the literary incarnation of Godhead. Śrīla Prabhupāda teaches that Vyāsadeva is the supreme authority on all Vedic literatures, having divided the original Veda into four divisions and compiled the Mahābhārata, Purāṇas, and Vedānta-sūtra.
