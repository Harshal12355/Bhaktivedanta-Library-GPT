# Karma and Accepting Different Types of Bodies

> The process of transmigration, where the eternal soul accepts various physical vessels, is a fundamental teaching in the philosophy of Kṛṣṇa consciousness. Śrīla Prabhupāda explains that this continuous change of body is not accidental but is strictly determined by the living entity's karma and their specific psychological disposition at the time of death.
