# Why Are There So Many Varieties of Life - The Science of Karma and Reincarnation

> Śrīla Prabhupāda consistently challenges the limitations of modern education by asking a simple yet profound question: why is there such a vast diversity of species in the material world? The Vedic wisdom reveals that the 8,400,000 species are not random evolutionary accidents, but rather precisely designed physical vehicles awarded to the eternal soul based on its past actions and desires.
