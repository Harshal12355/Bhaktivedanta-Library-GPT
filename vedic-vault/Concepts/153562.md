# Bhakti-yoga - The Ultimate Goal of All Yoga Systems

> In the vast library of Vedic knowledge, various systems of yoga are described to help the conditioned soul reconnect with the Absolute Truth. Śrīla Prabhupāda clarifies that while these different paths—such as karma-yoga, jñāna-yoga, and haṭha-yoga—offer specific benefits and elevations, they are not independent ends in themselves. Rather, they are progressive steps on a "yoga ladder" that ultimately culminates in bhakti-yoga, or pure devotional service to the Supreme Personality of Godhead, Kṛṣ
