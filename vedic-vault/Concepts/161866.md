# Learning the ABCD of Spiritual Knowledge through Bhagavad-gita

> Just as a child cannot understand advanced literature without first learning the alphabet, a conditioned soul cannot understand the profound mysteries of the Absolute Truth without first mastering the basics. As Śrīla Prabhupāda confirmed by the foundational texts like Bhagavad-gītā 2.13, the true beginning of wisdom lies not in mundane academic degrees, but in the realization of the eternal spirit soul.
